Selected work: should a European league enter India?

A market-entry case I wrote with Geneva Business School faculty, based on my own survey and interviews.

The question was whether EuroLeague Basketball, which has almost no presence in India, should enter the market, and how. I designed and ran a survey of 111 fans and interviewed four senior people from EuroLeague's digital, hospitality and competitions teams and from Indian basketball.

I weighed four options: a digital-first launch, partnerships with schools and academies, pilot events, or waiting. The evidence pointed to phased entry: mobile-first, local-language content, built over years rather than around one-off events.

The same method works for any market-entry or customer question: get primary data, check it against expert views, and set out the options with their trade-offs.

111 respondents, mostly urban and aged 18–34, reached through basketball networks. Shows early-adopter demand; not nationally representative.
