About me

Built on court.
Sharpened in the office.

Process, people
and follow-through.

In a government office, a wrong record or a missed deadline is somebody's career. I spent four years making sure neither happened, for 130+ people.

At the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs in Pune, I kept service records, coordinated the annual staff transfer process, onboarded new joiners and handled RTI requests and confidential files. At EuroLeague Basketball I recorded data from clubs across Europe and coordinated with their staff day to day. I also hold a master's in international management, a Six Sigma Green Belt and the Google Project Management certificate.

I'm looking for roles in operations, administration, sales development, customer support and research, in any industry. Before all this I played basketball competitively, which is where the discipline comes from.

Sport gave me discipline. Government work taught me process and paperwork that has to be right. EuroLeague showed me how a top competition runs day to day.
